## 1. Is there an upper limit? Yes — three of them

You asked directly, so here is a direct answer.

**Ceiling 1 — the addressable-question ceiling. This is the binding one.**
Peec tracks 36 prompts across 5 topics. The number of genuinely distinct pages that can be
retrieved for those is roughly **60–80**. Ditto already publishes **130 collection items +
55 blog posts = 185 pages**. Adding 44 more in two months does not extend coverage; past
roughly the 70-page mark on a topic, new pages compete with your own for the same slot.
Your own retrieval data shows the squeeze: 185 pages, 15 ever retrieved.

**Ceiling 2 — the retrieval ceiling.** AI engines cite few sources per answer. Across 323
tracked responses, only 15 distinct Ditto URLs were ever pulled. Being *the* page for
"documents requis pour le questionnaire EcoVadis" beats being one of six pages that
partially answer it. Depth wins slots; breadth does not.

**Ceiling 3 — the production ceiling.** 2/day media + 1/day site = 132 pieces over 44 working
days, ×2 languages = **264 documents**. At The Scope's own quality bar — named authors, cited
primary sources, industry-qualified angles, the anti-slop rules in `TONE-OF-VOICE.md` — that
is not reachable without dropping the bar. And thin content is not neutral: it dilutes the
topical signal on the pages you *do* want retrieved.

**So: the media cadence is fine. The Ditto cadence is not.**

The Scope is at article 36 of a planned 200. It is land-grabbing a genuinely uncovered beat
(QHSE + supply chain for the 250–1,000-employee practitioner), and 2/day is a reasonable
build-out rate for a new publication with a written plan behind it. Keep it.

The Ditto site is at 185 pages against a 5-topic demand surface. One new article per day
there is **the wrong instrument**. Which brings us to your other question.

## 2. On "improve rather than write" — yes, and it should be the majority

You asked what I think about recommendations that improve existing articles instead of adding
new ones. On the Ditto side this is not just valid, it is where most of the return is. Three
reasons grounded in the data above:

1. **You have high-traffic pages that AI engines never retrieve.**
   `en/collection/ecovadis/ecovadis-certified-companies-list` gets 458 views/90d — 3× the next
   page — and appears in **zero** Peec retrievals. It has demonstrated demand and no AI
   presence. Restructuring one page like that is worth more than five new ones.

2. **Your traffic is English, your tracked demand is French.** Top pages by views are EN;
   all 36 Peec prompts are FR. Several top EN pages have thinner FR twins. Bringing the FR
   side to parity is pure upside on the exact queries being measured.

3. **Improvement compounds on pages that already have authority.** A page already retrieved
   once is a page the engines have accepted. `documents-questionnaire-ecovadis` at 5
   retrievals is closer to 15 than a new page is to 1.

**Recommended split for the 44 Ditto slots: 18 new / 26 improvement.** The 18 new ones are
not "more of the same" — they are the pages for the two topics you have *nothing* for
(QHSE, ESG reporting software), plus the pricing/help-centre/buyer-guide pages Peec names
explicitly. Every one of the 26 improvement slots is tied to a page with either measured
traffic or a measured retrieval.

If you want the full 44 as new articles, the calendar below still works — take the 26
improvement rows as new-article briefs on the same topics. I would not, and the reason is
ceiling 1.

## 6. How to measure this

Two properties, two jobs, two scorecards. Judging The Scope on Peec visibility will make you
kill it prematurely; judging the Ditto site on brand reach will let it drift.

**Ditto site — Peec, reviewed fortnightly:**
- Visibility 3.7% → **8%** by 2 October (the QHSE and ESG-software blocks address 44% of tracked prompts, currently returning zero)
- Distinct retrieved URLs 15 → **35**
- Retrieved URLs on QHSE prompts 0 → **≥4**
- Citation rate on `documents-questionnaire-ecovadis` 0.60 → **≥1.5**
- Keep position ≤3.5 (currently 3.2 — do not trade quality for volume)

**The Scope — reviewed at 90 days, not before:**
- Indexation and first organic sessions (expect an 8–12 week lag from today's launch)
- Retrieval of *any* `/media/` URL — the leading indicator that the beat is landing
- Then, and only then, visibility contribution

**Kill criteria, agreed up front:** if by 2 October the Ditto-site blocks have moved
visibility by less than 1.5 points, the constraint is not volume — it is that the pages are
not structured for extraction. The response is to rewrite for extractability, not to publish
more. Re-read ceiling 2 before adding pages.
