# The Scope — the first 200 articles

A publication plan for the industry-by-framework grid, built after reviewing three reference
media (evoqse-plus.fr, blog-qhse.com, greenly.earth/leaf-media) and The Scope's own editorial
line. Every taxonomy node gets coverage; the 16 already-published articles (carbon footprint
by industry, EcoVadis scorecard, ISO 45001 getting started, REACH 2026 checklist, physical
vs monetary method) are not repeated.

**What the reference sites taught us.** Evoqse+ wins on practical QHSE service pages (DUERP,
MASE, HACCP) with zero editorial voice — we beat it with industry-qualified reference pieces.
Blog-QHSE covers the widest regulatory beat (NIS2, TNFD, PDCA, CSRD) but generically — we
beat it with the industry axis and named authors. Greenly's Leaf Media owns carbon content
volume — we do not chase its news cadence; we win on the two pillars it ignores (QHSE, supply
chain) and on the practitioner angle.

**Format key.** Each entry: title · format (Reference/Feature) · author · date.
Second line: `section path` · industries (empty = all industries). Then the angles to cover
and the sources to work from. Dates are spread between 2026-01-30 and 2026-07-30 as
requested. Roughly 1 in 10 pieces is a Feature, per the editorial line.

**Authors.** Pierre Masse (retail, distribution, cosmetics, EcoVadis, purchasing) ·
Tristan Salaün (tech, software, electronics, evidence) · Ugo Le Borgne (transport, aviation,
manufacturing, carbon, data collection) · Alexis de Taillac (CSRD, regulation, frameworks) ·
Pierre Poirmeur (features, strategy) · Claire Sikorav (by invitation, CSRD practice).
